PATROL MANUAL

How to play Mona Patrol

Drive Mona's six-wheel lunar rover across fivecheckpoint sectors (A–E, F–J, K–O, P–T, and U–Z). Every reached checkpoint acts as a safe respawn beacon if you lose one of your three buggies.

Keyboard

Enter or Space starts. ← / → or A / D shifts between Slow, Cruise, and Boost. Space, ↑, or W jumps. Z, X, or Ctrl fires forward and anti-air cannons together. P pauses and M toggles audio.

Touch & pointer

Tap Slower or Faster below the screen to change rover speed, tap Jump to clear craters and mines, and tap or hold Fire to clear boulders and overhead bug saucers.

Hazards & scoring

  • Merge-conflict craters (<<<): cannot be shot — time your jump arc to clear them (+100 to +150 pts).
  • Lunar boulders: jump over them or blast small rocks (1 hit, +100 pts) and tall fractured stacks (2 hits, +200 pts).
  • Bug saucers overhead: shoot them with your upward turret (+200 to +300 pts, +500 wave clear) and intercept falling regression bombs (+100 pts).
  • Sector clear bonus: finish major checkpoints (E, J, O, T, Z) under the 40-second par time for 1,000+ bonus points.
